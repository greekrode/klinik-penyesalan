## Design Context

### Users
Indonesian retail investors and traders using Klinik Penyesalan's market tools and community. This campaign introduces the Capital Market Masterclass Series 2026 and directs interested visitors to the supplied Lynk checkout.

### Brand Personality
Credible, direct, editorial. The creator's supplied campaign posters establish charcoal, teal, paper textures, and expert-led education. Avoid promises of investment returns and invented urgency.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the supplied speaker posters intact in a directly operated carousel. Carry through the site's Space Grotesk typography and teal brand accent; use restrained monospace for metadata. Use a centered first-visit modal with generous surrounding space and a lightly dimmed backdrop so the homepage remains visible, while background interaction stays blocked. Keep individual and student prices visible; put group pricing behind an accessible disclosure button. Keep registration and a secondary contact button side by side in the pinned footer.

### Design Principles
1. Preserve the supplied event facts, prices, speaker identities, and image artwork.
2. Establish hierarchy with strong typography and whitespace, not extra decorative cards.
3. Show the promotion once per campaign per browser; dismissal should be obvious.
4. Make checkout, keyboard navigation, and mobile layouts straightforward.
5. Keep previews isolated from production and distinguish checkout clicks from purchases.
6. On mobile, show a compact header, then the full carousel artwork, then two price columns with struck-through regular prices and exact savings. Explicitly compare the student rate to the regular individual rate. Preserve visible homepage space above and below the modal; allow internal scrolling on short screens and when group prices expand.
